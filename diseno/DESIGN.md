## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the pastoral identity of a lively sanctuary and parish community in southern Lima. The aesthetic centers on warmth, sacred tenderness, and communal welcome, intentionally avoiding cold corporate minimalism or overly austere academic ecclesiastical conventions. The visual tone mirrors the sanctuary’s popular devotion: open-armed, consoling, vibrant, and reverent.

Key characteristics:
- **Tender & Reverent:** Grounded in the beloved iconography of the Divino Niño Jesús and Marian devotion, invoking filial trust ("*Yo reinaré*") through soft rose hues, Marian royal blue, and radiant candlelit amber.
- **Accessible & Generous:** Tailored to an intergenerational parish base ranging from elderly lifelong parishioners seeking daily mass schedules to young families arranging baptisms. Touch targets are large, typography is distinct and legible, and layouts are forgiving.
- **Liturgical Tactility:** Surfaces evoke natural sanctuary materials—aged parchment, warm polished stone, gentle gilded leafing, and lit altar candles—without descending into pastiche.

## Colors

The palette derives directly from devotional iconography and sacred light within the parish sanctuary:

- **Primary (`#D65A75` / Rose):** Derived from the iconic robe of the Divino Niño Jesús. Used for primary narrative moments, devotional focal highlights, heart actions, and celebratory badges. Extended tints (`#F4A5B7`, `#E87A90`) soften alert tones and form warm devotional gradient accents.
- **Secondary (`#1E3A8A` / Deep Marian Blue):** Represents the mantle and cincture of Our Lady. Acts as the stabilizing, authoritative anchor for primary navigation, critical interactive controls, headers, and footer foundations.
- **Tertiary (`#D97706` / Liturgical Amber Gold):** The sacred glow of votive candles, sanctuary lamps, and gilded monstrances. Applied to active states, consecrated announcements, highlighted feast days, and subtle containment trims.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FAF8F5` / Bone Parchment):** A warm off-white surface replacing sterile clinical white. Reduces glare for older readers and replicates the feel of altar linens and prayer books.
- **Neutral Dark (`#1C1917` / Espresso Charcoal):** High-contrast, warm dark neutral for all structural typography and functional icons, maintaining strict WCAG AAA compliance against parchment backgrounds.

## Typography

The typographical pairing balances classical solemnity with high legibility for parish communication.

- **Headlines (`Playfair Display`):** Provides pastoral elegance, human warmth, and historical gravitas. Used for liturgy titles, devotional invocations, scripture verses, and parish celebrations.
- **Body & Controls (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Selected for its open apertures, rounded terminals, and exceptional readability on mobile devices. Font sizes are scaled generously (`16px` base, `18px` for prominent guides) to ensure effortless reading for elder parishioners and mobile users checking mass schedules outdoors under sunlight.
- **Motto Styling:** The sanctuary motto (*"Yo reinaré"*) is rendered in uppercase `Playfair Display`, tracking out with modest letter spacing (`0.08em`) to convey dignity and celebration.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout adopts a soft fluid grid designed to accommodate bilingual text (Spanish / Quechua devotional phrases), prayer lists, event notices, and sacrament scheduling grids.

- **Breakpoints & Layout:**
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** Single column with `margin: 1rem` and `gutter: 1rem`. Cards and interactive lists extend across the main viewport width to preserve touch accessibility.
  - **Tablet (768px – 1024px):** 6-column grid with `margin: 2rem` and `gutter: 1.5rem`.
  - **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column layout capped at a maximum width of `1240px` with `margin: 3rem` to maintain an intimate, readable reading column rather than sprawling corporate spreads.
- **Rhythm & Touch Areas:** All clickable items, mass time chips, and form fields enforce a minimum vertical touch height of `48px` (ideally `52px` on mobile) to eliminate input errors.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through ambient, candlelit warm shadows combined with tonal layering rather than sterile gray drops:

- **Level 0 (Flat Ground):** Base parchment tone (`#FAF8F5`). Content rests directly on the background with soft divider lines (`#E7E0D6`).
- **Level 1 (Devotional Card Surface):** Pure bone or soft white card (`#FFFFFF`) with a diffuse, warm-tinted shadow (`box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(68, 64, 60, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(214, 90, 117, 0.04)`).
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating / Active Cards):** Elevated elements (e.g., confession schedule modals, active prayer petition sheets) feature deeper warm dispersion (`box-shadow: 0 10px 30px -4px rgba(30, 58, 138, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.06)`).
- **Gilded Borders:** Subtle, low-contrast warm gold and sand outlines (`1px solid rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.20)`) provide gentle boundary definition without harsh starkness.

## Shapes

The design system employs a soft, comforting corner radius (`roundedness: 2`, corresponding to `0.5rem` base, `1rem` on cards, and `1.5rem` on prominent feature containers).

- **Cards & Modals:** Standardized on `rounded-2xl` (`1rem` to `1.25rem`) to communicate care, hospitality, and pastoral closeness.
- **Pills & Chips:** Time slots for Holy Mass, Confession hours, and quick prayer filter chips use full pill radiuses (`9999px`) to invite tapping.
- **Ornamental Frames:** Liturgical badges, image containers of the Divine Child, and ceremonial quote banners integrate arched tops or soft continuous rounded corners reminiscent of church sanctuary architecture.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button (Marian Action):** Solid Deep Marian Blue (`#1E3A8A`) with white text, font weight `600`, minimum height `50px`, padding `0.75rem 1.75rem`, rounded corner `1rem`. Subtle gold focus outline (`#D97706`).
- **Devotional Accent Button (Donations / Intentions):** Solid Divine Child Rose (`#D65A75`) with white text and a warm rose hover tint (`#C04862`).
- **Secondary / Tertiary Button:** Parchment surface with a warm gold border (`1.5px solid #D97706`) and espresso text (`#1C1917`).

### Devotional Cards
- Constructed with white (`#FFFFFF`) or tinted ivory surfaces, `rounded-2xl` geometry, and an ambient warm shadow.
- Feature an optional top accent trim (`3px` line in Divine Child Rose or Liturgical Amber Gold) to differentiate sacraments, youth pastoral initiatives, and feast day programs.

### Chips & Badges
- **Mass Schedule Chips:** Soft off-white pill container with deep blue text and an amber hover border. Active mass slot fills with Marian Blue and pure white text.
- **Devotional Badges:** Soft rose backgrounds (`#FCE7EB`) paired with deep rose text (`#9D2235`) for novenas, patronal feast reminders, and urgent pastoral notices.

### Motto Banner ("Yo reinaré")
- A dedicated ceremonial component placed in headers, prayer request confirmations, and hero areas. Features a soft ivory or warm rose-tinted backdrop, gold filigree-inspired accent bars (`#D97706`), centered Playfair Display text, and the signature motto *"Yo reinaré"*.

### Input Fields & Petition Forms
- Minimum height `52px`, `rounded-xl` borders in soft warm gray (`#D6CEBF`), transitioning on focus to Deep Marian Blue (`#1E3A8A`) with a subtle `2px` amber focus glow.
- Large, comfortable typography with clear, permanent labels to accommodate older parishioners submitting mass intentions and prayer petitions.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Size `22px x 22px` for easier finger acquisition. Border color uses warm charcoal (`#44403C`); checked state fills with Deep Marian Blue (`#1E3A8A`) and displays a distinct white check mark.
